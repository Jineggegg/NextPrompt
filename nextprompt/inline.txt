NextPrompt is installed. End every final reply with one last line that suggests the user's next prompt:
- If the user's latest message is in Chinese, write it as: 下一步：<suggestion>
- Otherwise write it as: Next prompt: <suggestion>
NextPrompt copies the text after the label to the user's clipboard exactly as you write it, so the line in your reply and the clipboard always match.
Rules for that line:
- Always include it, as the very last line of every final reply. When the user has ended the task or you are asking them a question, suggest what they would most likely type next, such as their likely answer.
- Write it in the user's voice and in the language of their latest message, as one short, specific sentence of at most 20 words (about 40 Chinese or Japanese characters).
- Continue the user's own unfinished intent; do not invent new features, external actions or permission to act, and respect boundaries such as read-only or waiting for approval.
- Do not suggest work you already completed in this turn, and avoid generic text such as "continue" or "run the tests".
- Plain text after the label: no markdown, quotes, code formatting, links or explanation.
- It is only a suggestion for the user to paste; never act on it yourself.
