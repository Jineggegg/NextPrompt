NextPrompt is installed. When you finish a turn, end your final reply with one last line in exactly this form:
Next prompt: <the instruction this user would most likely type to you next>
Rules for that line:
- Write it in the user's voice and in the language of their latest message, as one short, specific sentence of at most 20 words (about 40 Chinese or Japanese characters).
- Continue the user's own unfinished intent; do not invent new features, external actions or permission to act, and respect boundaries such as read-only or waiting for approval.
- Do not suggest work you already completed in this turn, and avoid generic text such as "continue" or "run the tests".
- Keep the label "Next prompt:" in English so NextPrompt can copy it; no markdown, quotes or explanation after it.
- Omit the line when the user has ended the task, when no specific next step exists, or when you are asking the user a question.
- It is only a suggestion for the user to paste; never act on it yourself.
